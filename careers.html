Hiring process

What applicants can expect

The exact process can vary by position, but applicants may move through several stages as their qualifications and application are reviewed.

01

Application

Review available opportunities and submit the requested application information through the appropriate company hiring channel.

02

Application Review

Submitted information may be reviewed to determine whether an applicant's qualifications and experience align with the requirements of the position.

03

Interview

Selected applicants may be invited to an interview or other assessment related to the position and its responsibilities.

04

Review & Decision

The hiring team reviews relevant application and interview information before determining appropriate next steps.

05

Employment Offer

Successful candidates may receive formal information regarding the position, employment terms, and any required next steps.

06

Onboarding

New team members may complete required onboarding processes and receive information relevant to their position, team, responsibilities, and work arrangements.

Recruitment safety

Always verify that employment communications are connected to Golden Grove Harvest and its authorized hiring process.

Protect your information

Be cautious of unofficial websites, impersonators, or unexpected requests that do not match the company's established hiring process.
